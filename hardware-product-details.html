Installation Guide

1

Template Positioning

Use the provided template to mark mounting holes on door and frame. Ensure proper clearance for arm movement and door swing.

2

Mounting Installation

Secure the closer body to the door using provided screws. Install the bracket on the frame, ensuring proper alignment with the closer arm.

3

Adjustment & Testing

Connect the arm assembly and adjust closing force using the adjustment valve. Test door operation and fine-tune settings as needed.
